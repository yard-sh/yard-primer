Frequently asked questions

Is Primer free?

Yes. Every course marked Free is free to take, with your progress saved, as long as you're signed in. Premium courses need the Premium plan.

What does Premium include?

Every course in the catalog, Free and Premium, including new courses the day they are published. It's a monthly subscription; the price is in the Pricing section above.

Do I need an account?

You can browse every course and its list of sections without one. To read a section and save your progress, sign in with your Yard account.

How is my progress saved?

Opening a section marks it in progress, and you mark it complete when you've finished. Both are saved to your account, so the course picks up where you left off on any device.

How do I cancel Premium?

From your Yard library, where your subscription is listed. Payments and cancellations are handled by Yard.

Who writes the courses?

The team that runs this Primer. They write and edit every course and section from Primer's admin panel.
